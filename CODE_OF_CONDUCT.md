# Code of Conduct

This repository is maintained by a single operator and is not a community project.  
Contributions are welcome if they align with the project's goals and maintain its technical and security standards.

Please:
- Be respectful and professional in all interactions.
- Keep discussions focused on technical topics.
- Avoid sharing sensitive information or personal data.
- Do not use issues or pull requests for unrelated support or promotion.

Violations may result in blocked interactions or declined contributions.
