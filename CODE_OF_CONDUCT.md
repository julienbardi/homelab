# Code of Conduct

This repository is maintained by a single operator and is not a community project. Contributions are welcome only if they align with the project's technical goals and maintain its security standards.

### Expectations
To ensure a productive environment, please:
- Be respectful and professional in all interactions.
- Keep discussions focused on technical topics.
- **Never** share sensitive information, personal data, or secrets (API keys, passwords, etc.) in issues or pull requests.
- Do not use issues or pull requests for unrelated support, advertising, or promotion.

### Consequences
Violations of these guidelines may result in your interactions being blocked or your contributions being declined.
